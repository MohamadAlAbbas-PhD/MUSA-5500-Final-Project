About the data

  • DHS surveys (via IPUMS-DHS), 2000–2021: children aged 6–59 months tested for anemia (hemoglobin below 11 g/dL), and the share of households in each survey cluster with no toilet.
  • Malaria Atlas Project: P. falciparum prevalence in children aged 2–10, a 5 km raster for each survey year, averaged in a 2 km (urban) or 5 km (rural) buffer around each cluster.
  • CHIRPS rainfall: the 3 months before each survey compared with 1981–2010 (a z-score), plus typical annual rainfall.
  • geoBoundaries region borders.

Privacy. DHS does not allow publishing survey locations. This site shows only hexagons with 3 or more clusters and the open rasters. The analysis notebooks run offline on the author's computer.
